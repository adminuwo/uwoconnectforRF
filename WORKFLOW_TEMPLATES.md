**Trigger Keyword:** hi, hello, bank, banking

**Conversational Flow Highlights:**
- **WELCOME**: 🏦 Welcome to ABC Bank Digital Assistant.

- **MORE SERVICES 1**: ✨ Additional Banking Services (1/3):

  - *Options:* 📈 Investments, 📦 Fixed Deposits, ✨ More Services 3
- **MORE SERVICES 3**: ✨ Additional Banking Services (3/3):
  - *Options:* ✍ Cheque Services, ☎ Customer Support, 📍 Branch & ATM Locator
- **ACCT MENU**: 💳 Account Services
  - *Options:* Balance & Details, Account Statements, KYC & Profile Updates
- **ACCT BAL DETAILS**: 💳 Balance & Account Info:
  - *Options:* Balance Enquiry, Account Details, Back to Account Menu
- **ACCT BAL SUCCESS**: ✅ Verification Successful!
- **ACCT OTP ERROR**: ❌ Verification Failed.
  - *Options:* Resend OTP, Speak to Agent, Main Menu
- **ACCT STATEMENT MENU**: 📄 Account Statements:
  - *Options:* Mini Statement, Email Statement, Back
- **MINI STMT SUCCESS**: ✅ Mini Statement for A/c 4567:

  - *Options:* Last 3 Months, Last 6 Months, Financial Year
- **EMAIL STMT SUCCESS**: ✅ Your statement has been sent successfully to your registered email address.
- **KYC UPDATE MENU**: 👤 KYC & Profile Information:
  - *Options:* Update KYC Status, Change Address, Update Mobile
- **KYC DOC SELECT**: Select Document to Upload:

- **KYC SUCCESS**: ✅ Profile updates / KYC documents received!

- **DEBIT BLOCK SUCCESS**: 🔒 Card Action Successful!

- **DEBIT LIMIT OPTIONS**: Manage Debit Card Limits:

- **DEBIT LIMIT SUCCESS**: ✅ Limit Configuration Updated!
- **CREDIT CARD MENU**: 💳 Credit Card Services:
  - *Options:* Outstanding & Bills, Increase Limit, Block / EMI / Rewards

  - *Options:* Pay Total Outstanding, Pay Minimum Due, Back
- **CC PAY SUCCESS**: 💸 Payment Successful!
- **CC LIMIT INFO**: 🎉 Great News!
  - *Options:* Confirm Limit Increase, Cancel
- **CC LIMIT SUCCESS**: ✅ Credit Limit Increased!
- **CC OTHER OPTIONS**: Additional Credit Card Options:
  - *Options:* Block Credit Card, EMI Conversion, Rewards Points Balance
- **CC OTHER SUCCESS**: ✅ Request Registered Successfully!

  - *Options:* Home / Personal Loan, Car / Business Loan, Education / Gold Loan

  - *Options:* Home Loan, Personal Loan, Car Loan
- **LOAN ELIGIBLE SUCCESS**: 🎉 Pre-Approved Eligibility Confirmed!
- **PAYMENTS MENU**: 💸 Payments & Transfers
  - *Options:* UPI Instant Transfer, NEFT / RTGS / IMPS, International Transfer
