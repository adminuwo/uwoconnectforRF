## 9. Prepaid Usage Wallet & Razorpay Recharge Guide

UwoConnect features a **Prepaid Usage Wallet** designed for metered consumption (WhatsApp Meta conversation fees, AI API calls, and extra broadcast campaigns).

### 💡 Core Wallet Rules:
1. **Subscription is Separate**: Your ₹499/mo UwoConnect software subscription is managed separately and is **never** deducted from your usage wallet balance.
2. **100% Direct Credit**: 100% of your recharge amount (e.g. ₹500, ₹1,000, ₹2,000, ₹5,000) is credited directly to your usage balance without hidden fees or forced markups.

### 💳 How to Top-up Your Wallet:
1. Click **`₹6,000.00 + Topup`** in the top navigation header or **`Add Money`** on the Broadcast / Wallet page.
2. Select a preset amount (₹500, ₹1,000, ₹2,000, ₹5,000) or enter a custom amount.
3. Click **`PAY ₹1,000 & RECHARGE`**.
4. The **Official Razorpay Payment Gateway Window** pops up on your screen.
5. Choose your preferred payment method:
   * 🟢 **UPI App**: Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM (QR scan or UPI ID).
   * 💳 **Credit / Debit Card**: Visa, Mastercard, RuPay.
   * 🏦 **NetBanking**: SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis & 50+ Banks.
   * 👛 **Wallets**: Paytm, Mobikwik, PhonePe.
6. Complete payment in Razorpay $\rightarrow$ your wallet is credited instantly with an immutable transaction receipt!

### 📣 Broadcast Entitlements:
* **Included Broadcasts**: Your subscription plan includes free broadcasts per month.
* **Additional Broadcasts**: When included broadcasts are used up, extra broadcasts cost **₹0.94 from your wallet balance**.
* **Automatic Unlock**: Once your wallet has $\ge$ ₹0.94, additional broadcasts become **UNLOCKED & ENABLED**.
* **Zero Pre-Deduction**: Simply adding money to your wallet does **not** consume a broadcast. Balance is deducted only when you actually click **Send Broadcast**.
